i.
Gate of opening

Before the work begins, a threshold.

Diana Bürgin is a German conscious entrepreneur, Kambo practitioner, shamanic guide, and tantra teacher. She lives between Frankfurt am Main and Koh Phangan — a polarity that shapes the practice.

A graduate in International Business (Northumbria University and accadis Business School), she trained as a brand strategist at OgilvyBrains and the Peter Schmidt Group before stepping into her own work. She is the founder of Glowed.me — the Frankfurt-born beauty house honored as Best Beauty Newcomer at the 2024 Look! Beauty Awards — and of the brand consultancy Elevate Brands.

Across more than a decade of building, her work has consistently centered self-love, feminine confidence, authenticity, and the return to one's own radiance. The ceremonial practice now carries those currents inward — from outer beauty into the deeper field where the body becomes the teacher.

iii.
The three medicines

Three doorways into the same field — the body as altar.

i.kambo

Kambo

A ceremony of cleansing & renewal

A traditional process involving Phyllomedusa bicolor, long associated with cleansing, resilience, and the return of life force. Held with thorough preparation, health screening, informed consent, and integration on the other side.

Lineage · Matsés · Katukina
ii.tantra

Tantra

A path of embodied presence

With deep roots in South Asian spiritual traditions, tantra honors the body as a vessel of presence, vitality, intimacy, and intelligence. Taught here with reverence, precision, and unambiguous consent.

Lineage · Kashmir Shaivism · Sahaja
iii.healing space

Healing Space

A grounded container for integration

A calm, deliberate vessel for release, self-responsibility, emotional integration, and reconnection with the wisdom of the body. The work continues long after the ceremony ends.

Lineage · Somatic · Shamanic
iv.
The arc

A ceremony is a small sacred round — preparation, ceremony, return.

7 days before

Preparation

Intake conversation. Health screening. Quiet diet, gentle movement, intention-setting. The body is asked, not surprised.

A single day

Ceremony

The container opens. Kambo ritual or embodied tantra session, held with steady attention and the practitioner's full presence.

21 days after

Integration

Rest, reflection, follow-up. The medicine continues to work; the practice is to keep listening as it does.

07 — The signature offering

A single arc. Privately held.

Each container is designed end-to-end to support clarity, energetic release, emotional presence, and a deeper remembrance of the self. Whether held one-to-one or with a small group, the rhythm is the same: arrive, soften, surrender, return.

  1. 01 Preparation Intake, screening, intention-setting. 7 days
  2. 02 Ceremony Kambo ritual · embodied tantra · holding. 1 day
  3. 03 Integration Rest, reflection, follow-up conversation. 21 days
A care note

Kambo is intense practice. Please read carefully.

Kambo has documented physiological effects and risks. It is not a medical treatment, and is not a substitute for medical or psychological care.

Participation requires honest health screening, informed consent, and an awareness of contraindications. If you are unsure whether the practice is right for you at this moment, please reach out — sometimes the most honoring answer is not yet.
